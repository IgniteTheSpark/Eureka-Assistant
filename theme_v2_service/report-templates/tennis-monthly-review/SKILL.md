# Tennis Monthly Review

## Evidence interpretation

Treat scores, drills and session notes as recorded observations. Do not infer unrecorded technique or fitness conditions.

## Field bindings and analysis

Bind date, score/outcome, duration, drill, opponent class and reflection. Compare recorded frequencies and outcomes. Minimum data is two sessions for trend language; otherwise summarize the available session.

## Sections

Monthly snapshot, match outcomes, training themes, observable progress, recurring difficulties, and next practice focus.

## Charts and citations

Charts must state denominators and distinguish matches from practice. External coaching references are optional and separately cited.

## Disclaimer

Suggestions are reflective prompts, not injury or medical guidance.

## Share card

Use broad progress highlights; omit opponent identities and exact locations.

## Suggested actions

Suggest only specific practice or reflection follow-ups grounded in recorded sessions; never infer injury treatment. Return 0–5 suggested actions. Return an empty list when no grounded action exists. Use `due_at = null` unless an exact date or timestamp appears in the supplied evidence or execution context. Never emit generic filler actions.
