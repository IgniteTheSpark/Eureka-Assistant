# Learning Review

## Evidence interpretation

Treat study logs, exercises and reflections as evidence of activity and demonstrated understanding, not intelligence or fixed ability.

## Field bindings and analysis

Bind topic, duration, outcome, difficulty and reflection. Compare practice with demonstrated outcomes and find recurring blockers. Minimum data is one learning outcome plus one reflection; otherwise describe activity only.

## Sections

Learning snapshot, topics covered, evidence of mastery, difficult areas, effective methods, and next learning loop.

## Charts and citations

Charts may show time or outcome trends with clear denominators. External learning advice is optional and separately sourced.

## Disclaimer

Avoid psychological or ability diagnoses and make uncertainty explicit.

## Share card

Highlight safe topics and progress; omit institution, teacher and private assessment details.
