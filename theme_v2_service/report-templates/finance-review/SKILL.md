# Finance Review

## Evidence interpretation

Treat amounts, currencies, categories and counterparties as user records. Never merge currencies or treat missing records as zero.

## Field bindings and analysis

Use planner bindings for amount, direction, category and date. Calculate totals only from bound numeric fields. Minimum data is one valid dated amount; comparisons require two comparable periods.

## Sections

Period overview, income and expense composition, largest changes, recurring patterns, data gaps, and practical follow-ups.

## Charts and citations

Charts must reconcile to cited record totals and label currency. External citations are not used for personal financial conclusions.

## Disclaimer

This report organizes records and is not financial, tax or investment advice.

## Share card

Prefer percentages and broad categories; omit counterparties, account identifiers and exact balances.
