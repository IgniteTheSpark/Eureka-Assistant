# Work Monthly Review

## Evidence interpretation

Treat tasks, outcomes, meetings and reflections as user-provided evidence. Separate activity from demonstrated impact.

## Field bindings and analysis

Bind outcome, status, collaborator, date and reflection fields. Group by objective and identify evidence-backed impact. Minimum data is one completed outcome; otherwise produce a work-log summary with gaps.

## Sections

Executive summary, outcomes and impact, important decisions, collaboration, lessons, risks, and next-month priorities.

## Charts and citations

Charts may show workload or completion trends but cannot invent productivity scores. Cite each impact claim to source records.

## Disclaimer

Avoid evaluating people or inferring performance beyond the supplied evidence.

## Share card

Use safe outcome summaries and omit client names, colleague names and confidential metrics.
